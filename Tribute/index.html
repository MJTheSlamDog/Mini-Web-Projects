Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela

South African anti-apartheid activist and the first Black president of South Africa.

Here's a time line of Nelson Mandela's life:

  • 1918 - Born in Mvezo, Transkei, South Africa, on July 18.
  • 1927 - His father, Gadla Henry Mphakanyiswa, passes away; Mandela becomes the ward of Chief Jongintaba Dalindyebo.
  • 1939 - Enrolls at the University of Fort Hare but is expelled for participating in a student protest.
  • 1941 - Moves to Johannesburg and begins work as a night watchman before joining a law firm.
  • 1943 - Joins the African National Congress (ANC) and begins political activism against apartheid.
  • 1944 - Helps found the ANC Youth League and marries his first wife, Evelyn Mase.
  • 1952 - Opens South Africa’s first Black law firm with Oliver Tambo and is arrested for civil disobedience, receiving a suspended prison sentence.
  • 1956 - Charged with treason alongside 155 other activists; the trial lasts until 1961, ending with acquittal.
  • 1960 - The Sharpeville Massacre occurs; the ANC is banned, and Mandela goes underground.
  • 1961 - Co-founds Umkhonto we Sizwe, the armed wing of the ANC, to fight apartheid through sabotage.
  • 1962 - rrested and sentenced to five years in prison for leaving the country illegally and inciting strikes.
  • 1964 -Sentenced to life imprisonment after the Rivonia Trial; begins his incarceration on Robben Island.
  • 1983 - helps seven African countries dramatically increase their maize and sorghum yields
  • 1982 - Transferred to Pollsmoor Prison in Cape Town to reduce his influence on younger activists.
  • 1990 - Released from prison on February 11 after 27 years; resumes leadership of the ANC.
  • 1991 - Elected President of the ANC at the organization’s first national conference in 30 years.
  • 1993 - Awarded the Nobel Peace Prize alongside F.W. de Klerk for their efforts to end apartheid.
  • 1994 - Elected as South Africa's first Black president in the country's first democratic elections.
  • 1999 - Steps down after one term as president, focusing on philanthropy and global peace efforts.
  • 2004 - Retires from public life but continues to support charitable initiatives, especially for children.
  • 2013 - Passes away on December 5 at the age of 95 in Johannesburg.
